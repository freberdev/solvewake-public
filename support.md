## Common questions

### The alarm rang again after I pressed Stop
That's how SolveWake works: it rings again every minute until you've solved the problems. Tap **Solve** on the alarm to open the app and solve them.

### How do I stop an alarm in an emergency?
On the solve screen, press and hold **Emergency stop** for 5 seconds. The alarm stops for this time. Your upcoming alarms are not affected.

### The alarm didn't ring
- Check that alarms are allowed: Settings → Apps → SolveWake.
- Check that the alarm is switched on in the app.
- Check that your free trial or purchase is active. When the trial has ended, alarms pause until you unlock the app.

### The alarm is too quiet
SolveWake uses your iPhone's ringer volume, which you set in Settings → Sounds & Haptics. Tap **Test alarm** when editing an alarm to hear the real volume. Tip: turn off **Change with Buttons** in Settings → Sounds & Haptics so the volume buttons can't lower it by mistake.

### How does snooze work?
If snooze is on for an alarm, you solve one extra problem to snooze for 5 minutes. You can snooze at most once or twice, depending on the alarm's setting.

### What is the wake-up check?
Five minutes after you've solved the problems, a notification asks if you're awake. If you don't answer within a minute, the alarm rings again and you solve one more problem.

### Free trial and purchase
SolveWake is free for 3 days. After that your alarms pause until you unlock the app with a one-time purchase. There is no subscription. To restore a purchase, open **Settings** in the app and tap **Restore purchase**.

### Refunds
Purchases are handled by Apple. You can request a refund at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

### Requirements
iPhone with iOS 26.1 or later.
