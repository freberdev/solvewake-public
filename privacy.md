# Privacy Policy

<p class="updated">Last updated: 28 September 2026</p>

SolveWake is an alarm clock for iPhone made by freber dev AB ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

<div class="card"><p><strong>In short:</strong> SolveWake does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Everything the app knows stays on your iPhone.</p></div>

## Information stored on your iPhone

To work, the app saves the following **only on your device**:

- your alarms: times, repeat days, labels and settings
- your mornings: when an alarm rang, how long it took to solve the problems, wrong answers, snoozes and emergency stops
- whether your free trial or purchase is active

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes it. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the app's data may be included in that backup like any other app data. Backups are handled by Apple.

## No accounts, tracking or ads

- There are no accounts and no sign-in.
- There are no analytics, tracking or advertising tools, and no third-party code that collects data.
- The app does not access your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
- We do not run any servers for the app, so there is nowhere for your data to go.

## Permissions the app asks for

- **Alarms:** lets SolveWake schedule alarms with iOS so they ring even in Silent mode and Focus. The alarms are stored and triggered by your iPhone.
- **Notifications:** only asked for if you turn on the wake-up check. The notification is created and shown by your iPhone itself.

You can change these permissions at any time in the iPhone Settings app.

## Purchases

The free trial and the one-time purchase are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app only asks Apple, on your device, whether a purchase is active. Apple's [privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/) applies to purchases.

## Information Apple may share with us

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may give us aggregated usage statistics and crash reports. They do not identify you. You can turn this off in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

## Children

SolveWake does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

Because we do not process personal data about you through the app, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you email us, we only use your address and message to answer you, and we delete the conversation when it is no longer needed. You can always ask us what we have saved about you.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
