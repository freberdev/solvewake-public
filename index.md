# The alarm stops when you've solved the math.

SolveWake is an alarm clock for iPhone. To turn off the alarm you solve a few quick math problems. Press Stop without solving them and it rings again a minute later.

- Rings in Silent mode and Focus, even when the app is closed.
- Easy, medium or hard problems, 1–5 per alarm. No multiple choice.
- A wake-up check makes sure you stay up.
- No ads, no subscription, no data collection.
